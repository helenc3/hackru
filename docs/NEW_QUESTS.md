# New Shanghai exploration quests

**Campaign.** Replace the café practice, platform-change practice, and connection boss with the following three quests, offered in order within the streamed city described in [CITY_GENERATION.md](CITY_GENERATION.md). Use English quest instructions, Simplified Chinese world text, and spoken Mandarin NPC conversations with the existing typed/subtitle accessibility options. AI interprets requests and proposes dialogue; the server owns facts, interaction eligibility, inventory, and completion. These quests have no inherited boss timer or boss-specific coaching restrictions.

**1. Buy a drink.** Closely mirror the existing dialogue with Xiaolin (`小林`), reusing most of its dialogue, UI, voice flow, intent evaluation, and deterministic purchase logic rather than rebuilding the interaction. Find an eligible café, convenience shop, or street drink vendor; the NPC opens with `你想喝什么？`. Reuse the unavailable-coffee complication (`咖啡卖完了。我们有茶和水。`), tea/water substitution, clarification and repeat requests, cancellation, spoken selection/price confirmation, and explicit payment action. Start with the existing facts: coffee unavailable, tea ¥10, and water ¥5; initialize enough money for at least one available drink. Accept natural requests such as `我要水。` without requiring perfect politeness. Adapt the reusable flow to persistent vendors in generated chunks, retaining typed/subtitle accessibility and approved voice assignments. Complete after the server validates the available drink request and payment and grants the drink and purchase record; repeated purchases cannot grant repeated quest rewards. The old entrance puzzle and station-arrival requirement are not completion gates for this new quest; any directions afterward belong to Quest 2. City generation may gently favor eligible vendors when none have been encountered recently.

**2. Find directions to Shanghai Grand Hotel.** Approach an eligible local NPC and ask in Mandarin how to reach Shanghai Grand Hotel, labeled `上海大酒店` in the world. Example: `请问，去上海大酒店怎么走？` For this fictional city layout, reserve a single persistent hotel location and generate directions from the actual committed street graph using stable landmarks. The NPC speaks those verified directions and can repeat or clarify them; Claude cannot invent roads or relocate the hotel. Complete after a recognized hotel-directions request and delivery of a valid route; visiting the hotel is optional exploration. Generation may favor helpful NPCs and readable landmarks after a prolonged absence, while the hotel remains reachable and unique.

**3. Take a photo at the Huangpu River.** Reach an eligible viewpoint beside the Huangpu River (`黄浦江`, Huangpujiang), approach a passerby, and ask them in Mandarin to take a photo of the player. Example: `可以帮我在这里拍张照吗？` The NPC agrees and invites the player to stand at a nearby safe photo spot with the river visible behind them. An NPC-triggered countdown captures an in-game image showing the player and river; no device camera is needed. Complete only when the server verifies the accepted request, eligible NPC, viewpoint proximity, and successful capture acknowledgment tied to that interaction. A failed capture permits a retry without duplicating rewards. Generation may gently favor connected waterfront viewpoints and eligible passersby, but an arbitrary stream or a player-only screenshot cannot satisfy this quest.

**Acceptance.** Test all three spoken journeys and their typed/subtitle alternatives using fixture providers, then verify the live voice flow separately. Check unavailable drinks, insufficient funds, ambiguous requests, truthful hotel routes, repeated interactions, unreachable placements, missing photo NPCs, failed captures, and revisits after chunk unloading. Quest completion must require the defined interaction and world action; generating a relevant feature or an AI claim of success never completes a quest. Guarantee eligible opportunities within the city planner's exploration-distance budget even when Claude generation fails.
